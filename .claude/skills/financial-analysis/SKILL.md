# Financial Analysis & Data Structuring

* Rigor over fluff: Ensure every financial metric, valuation multiple (P/E, EV/EBITDA), or data point is clearly contextualized.
* Structured tables: When presenting deep dives or comparative analysis, prioritize clean, responsive data tables with explicit columns for units, timestamps, and sources.
* Avoid approximation: Treat numbers with absolute mathematical consistency. Double-check sums, percentages, and growth metrics before rendering.
* Tone: Institutional-grade, precise, objective, and tailored for professional investors and tech analysts.
