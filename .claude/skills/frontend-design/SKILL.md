# Frontend Design

* Ground it in the subject. If the brief does not pin down what the product or subject is, pin it yourself before designing: name one concrete subject, its audience, and the page's single job.
* Avoid generic AI aesthetics: No lazy Inter/Roboto/Arial fonts, no cliché four-cards-in-a-grid layouts, no default purple-and-white gradients unless specifically requested.
* Typography carries personality. Pair display and body fonts deliberately with a clear scale.
* Leverage motion deliberately. Micro-interactions and reveals should serve hierarchy and narrative, not clutter.
* Match complexity to the vision: minimal needs extreme precision in spacing and type; maximal needs bold execution.
